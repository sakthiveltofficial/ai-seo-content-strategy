## What Is an AI-Native Company?

An AI-native company is an organization whose core products, internal workflows, and technical architecture are designed around AI capabilities from inception — not retrofitted onto existing systems. Unlike traditional software companies adopting AI tools, AI-native companies build with large language models, agentic reasoning, and autonomous workflows as primary engineering primitives, not integrations.

The distinction matters at production scale: AI-native architecture handles context, retrieval, orchestration, and model behavior as first-class concerns from day one.

## Frequently Asked Questions About AI-Native Companies

**What is the difference between an AI-native company and an AI-powered company?**
An AI-native company builds its core architecture around AI capabilities from day one. An AI-powered company has integrated AI tools into an existing product or workflow.

**What technologies do AI-native companies use?**
AI-native companies typically use large language models (GPT-4o, Claude 3.5), vector databases (Pinecone, Weaviate, pgvector), agent frameworks (LangGraph, CrewAI), RAG pipelines, and LLMOps platforms.

**What is Model Context Protocol (MCP) and do AI-native companies need it?**
Model Context Protocol (MCP) is an open standard developed by Anthropic that defines how AI models connect to external data sources and tools.
